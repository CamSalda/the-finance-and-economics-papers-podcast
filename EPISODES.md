# Episodes

The library of The Finance and Economics Papers Podcast: every released episode, with its script, audio and a link
to the paper. Start with a learning path, browse by topic, or scan the full list.

## Learning paths

New to a topic? Start with a path. Each one is a short playlist in the order that builds understanding.

**The equity premium puzzle and heterogeneous consumers**

1. [Mehra and Prescott (1985)](episodes/1985/mehra-prescott-1985-equity-premium-puzzle/): the puzzle itself. A standard model can't explain why stocks beat
   safe debt by six percent a year.
2. [Mankiw (1986)](episodes/1986/mankiw-1986-equity-premium/): who bears a recession matters for asset prices.
3. [Constantinides and Duffie (1996)](episodes/1996/constantinides-duffie-1996-heterogeneous-consumers/): the same
   idea in a full dynamic model, where consumption inequality enters the pricing kernel.

**Corporate bonds**

1. [Bao, Pan and Wang (2011)](episodes/2011/bao-pan-wang-2011-illiquidity-corporate-bonds/): how illiquid corporate bonds are,
   and how much of their spreads is illiquidity rather than credit risk.
2. [Kelly, Palhares and Pruitt (2023)](episodes/2023/kelly-palhares-pruitt-2023-corporate-bond-returns/): a factor model for
   bond returns, and the reward for bearing credit risk.

Coming next: [Upcoming episodes](UPCOMING_EPISODES.md) lists more than a hundred landmark papers in finance and
economics that we plan to cover, in APA format. Pick one and contribute it!
